Institutional Governance & Executive Leadership

SETU Foundation operates under rigorous constitutional oversight, non-political bylaws, and fiduciary duty mandated by the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

Governance Statement

Ethical Stewardship and Legal Accountability

The Executive Committee functions as the supreme administrative body of SETU Foundation, entrusted by the General Assembly with the strategic stewardship of all ecological, technological, and emergency humanitarian programs across Bangladesh.

Quorum & Democratic Deliberation: Bi-monthly statutory meetings require a minimum two-thirds majority quorum for all budget expenditures and project ratifications.
Honorable Voluntary Service: All Executive Committee officers serve without financial salary, honorarium, or personal commercial interest to eliminate conflicts of interest.
Statutory Annual General Meeting (AGM): Mandated under Act XXI of 1860, our comprehensive audit reports and operational minutes are submitted annually to the RJSC Dhaka registrar.
