Pillar 01 · Ecological

EcoSETU

Afforestation & Climate Mitigation

Coastal Salinity-Resistant Mangrove Belts
School-Based Native Tree Nurseries
Urban Heat-Island Mitigation Audits

Pillar 02 · Technological

TechSETU

Digital Inclusion & Skills Bootcamps

Bridging the digital divide for students from underprivileged backgrounds through hands-on programming bootcamps, hardware recycling labs, and foundational AI literacy.

Free Community Python & Web Coding
Hardware Donation & Refurbishment Labs
Women in Tech Mentorship Pathways
Learn more about TechSETU

ReliefSETU

Disaster Response & Human Resilience

Rapid-dispatch emergency humanitarian aid during seasonal flash floods, severe riverbank erosion, and harsh winter cold waves across northern and northeastern Bangladesh.

Water Purification & Medical ORS Supply
Emergency Dry Food & Child Care Kits
Winter Warm Clothes Drive (Northern Belts)
